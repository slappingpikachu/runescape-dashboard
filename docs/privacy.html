Privacy Policy

OSRS Dashboard · Effective 11 November 2026

OSRS Dashboard runs on your own computer. Your game history is stored there, and the app has no server of its own, no account system, no analytics and no advertising. This policy explains what the app reads, what it sends over the internet, and what it does with Google Drive if you choose to connect it.

Data stored on your computer

  • Your player name and settings.
  • Hiscores snapshots, and the files the OSRS Data Sync RuneLite plugin saves on your computer: skills, quests, diaries, combat achievements, collection log, bank and other item containers, kill counts and drops.

This data stays on your computer unless you use one of the sync options below.

What the app downloads

When you press Update, the app downloads your public hiscores from Jagex, and item names, pictures and prices from the Old School RuneScape Wiki. If you choose the one-time import, it also downloads your public history from Wise Old Man. Each request sends only your player name or the item it is about.

Google Drive sync (optional)

If you click Connect Google Drive, you sign in to Google in your browser and allow the app access with one permission: drive.appdata, "see, create and delete its own configuration data in your Google Drive".

  • What the app accesses: only its own hidden application data folder in your Google Drive. It can't see, open or change any of your other Drive files, and it doesn't ask for your name, email address, contacts or anything else in your Google account.
  • What it stores there: one sync file per computer, holding the game history described above, labelled with your player name, the computer's name and the time it was saved.
  • How it is used: only to keep your own dashboards on your own computers up to date. The data goes directly between your computer and Google. It is never sent to the app's author or anyone else, and it is not used for advertising, sold or shared.
  • Sign-in token: Google gives the app a token so it can keep syncing without asking you to sign in again. The token is saved only on that computer, in the app's data folder, and is never put in a sync file.
  • Removing access: click Disconnect Google Drive in the app's Sync panel, which deletes the token and revokes it with Google. You can also remove the app at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. To delete the sync files themselves, open Google Drive's Settings, then Manage apps, and choose "Delete hidden app data" for OSRS Dashboard.

OSRS Dashboard's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Sync with a folder or a file

You can also sync by picking a folder that your computers share, or by exporting and importing a file yourself. The app then only reads and writes that folder or file; how it travels is up to you and the service you use.

Children

The app doesn't knowingly collect anything from anyone, and it has no accounts of its own.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new effective date. The history of every change is in the project's public source code.
